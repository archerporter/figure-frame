Methodology

Distal/Proximal ratio — at each frame, mean speed of wrist (lm15, lm16) and ankle (lm27, lm28) landmarks divided by mean speed of shoulder (lm11, lm12) and hip (lm23, lm24) landmarks. The summary value is the median across all frames. Values greater than 1× indicate that peripheral joints are moving faster than the body core — consistent with compensatory distal articulation.

X direction changes — sign reversals per second in the horizontal velocity of the centre of mass. A reversal is counted only when |vx| ≥ 0.08 (normalised units/s) to suppress noise.

Level changes — local minima and maxima per second in the centre-of-mass height above the estimated floor. The floor is computed as the 99th percentile of the maximum foot-landmark y-coordinate across all frames.

Landmark coordinates are MediaPipe normalised values (0–1 relative to frame dimensions). Velocities are therefore in frame-widths or frame-heights per second, not metric units.
